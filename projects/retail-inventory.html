Retail Inventory & Demand Forecasting

Power BI | Inventory Analysis | Demand Forecasting | Retail Analytics

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail inventory and demand across five branches, focusing on sales performance, inventory availability, stock shortages and demand patterns.

The analysis explores the relationships between actual sales, expected demand, inventory levels, discounts, competitor prices, weather and seasonality.

The dashboard presents inventory performance indicators, demand trends and analytical insights to support inventory planning and business decisions.
